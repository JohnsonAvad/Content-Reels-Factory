Compliance & Data Protection

1. Operation

This system serves as an Agentic Workflow Pipeline that transforms public-domain brand data into media. It is architected for cross-sector scalability (Retail, Health, Logistics, Finance).

2. Universal Identity Policy

The system does not maintain permanent caches of individual user data. All processing is Transient. Our "Human-in-the-loop" governance layer ensures brand safety for all connected partner organizations.

3. Data Revocation

All platform permissions granted by connected brands can be revoked instantly. Upon disconnection, our system performs an automated purge of associated access metadata from our local production workstation.
