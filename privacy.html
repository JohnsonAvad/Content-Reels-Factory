Privacy & Data Compliance

Last Updated: April 2026

1. Scope of Data Orchestration

The AVAD Content Orchestration Engine ("the System") functions as a transient media processing pipeline. We facilitate the transformation of public brand data (URLs) into structured media assets for Zion Business Venture and authorized retail partners.

2. Data Ingestion & Sovereignty

Our Ingestion Node harvests publicly available text and metadata from client-authorized URIs. We implement Privacy-by-Design: personally identifiable information (PII), such as names or private contact details, are programmatically filtered and excluded from the AI inference layer to maintain user anonymity and data sovereignty.

3. Platform API Integration (Meta & TikTok)

The System utilizes the Meta Graph API and TikTok Content Posting API solely for the delivery of generated media. We do not "cache" or store private user profile data. Access Tokens are stored in a secure, local environment vault and are used strictly for authorized binary uploads.

4. Data Retention & Deletion Protocol

Consistent with Meta and TikTok Platform Terms, users may revoke access at any time. To request a manual purge of any transient logs or to invoke your Right to be Forgotten, please follow the platform-side revocation process or contact our administrative head at: johnsonavad2023@gmail.com.

5. Compliance Standard

This policy is aligned with the Uganda Data Protection and Privacy Act and international ISO/IEC 27001 security standards, ensuring that East African enterprise data is handled with industrial-grade integrity.
