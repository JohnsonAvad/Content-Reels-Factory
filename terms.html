Terms of Service

1. Service Utilization

The AVAD Content Orchestration Engine provides high-velocity media synthesis services. By connecting your professional accounts, you authorize the system to perform automated narrative generation and asset distribution.

2. AI Integrity & Governance

The system utilizes Large Language Models (LLMs) to architect content. Every output is subject to a Human-in-the-loop (HITL) approval gate. Users retain full ownership and responsibility for final brand communication.

3. Data Deletion & Account Revocation

To ensure Data Sovereignty, we maintain a strict revocation policy. If you wish to delete your data or revoke our factory's access, follow these steps:

  • Navigate to your Platform Settings (Facebook/Instagram/TikTok).
  • Select 'Security and Apps'.
  • Locate 'AVAD Content Engine' and select 'Remove'.

Upon this action, our system performs an Atomic Cleanup: all transient access tokens and associated metadata are immediately purged from our local environment vault.
